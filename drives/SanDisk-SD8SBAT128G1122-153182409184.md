### Total Bad Block count of 142

Unchanged throughout the test; distinct from zero reassigned and grown bad-block counts. It may include manufacturing-excluded NAND blocks, but that definition is not verified for this exact firmware. Do not call this 142 unreadable Windows sectors or newly developed failures.

Formatting cannot repair worn NAND or erase its history. No repair utility or extra write cycle is indicated solely by this stable count. Monitor trends, reserve and real data verification; reassess if grown/reassigned counts or errors increase.
