### Raw read-error interpretation

Attribute 1's raw decimal value **10** (hex 0A) is unchanged. It must not be described as ten failed host reads or ten unreadable sectors without the firmware-specific encoding definition. Micron lists Raw Read Error Rate separately from Reported Uncorrectable Errors. The latter, along with pending and reallocated counts, remains zero.
