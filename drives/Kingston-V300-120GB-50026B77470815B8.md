## Manufacturer information and age

[Kingston V300 datasheet](https://www.kingston.com/datasheets/SV300S3_en.pdf):
- 2.5-inch, 7 mm; MLC NAND; customized LSI SandForce controller.
- 120 GB published endurance: **64 TBW**; original warranty three years.
- Published compressible sequential transfer: up to 450/450 MB/s read/write.
- Published incompressible baseline for 120 GB: 180/133 MB/s read/write.
- Operating temperature 0–70°C.
- Intended for desktop/notebook workloads; manufacturer did not position it as a server SSD.

[Kingston firmware notes](https://media.kingston.com/support/downloads/60AABBF0_V3_RN_052816.pdf) list installed revision **60AABBF0, May 28, 2016**, including this capacity. Notes address temporary unreadability/I/O freezing and SMART attribute 177 accuracy. No newer V300 firmware was found during the October 27 research; no update performed.

[Kingston discontinued-model listing](https://www.kingston.com/en/memory/search/discontinuedmodels?partid=SV300S37A%2F120G) lists replacement SA400S37/120G.

The V300 family was announced in November 2012: [contemporaneous reproduction of Kingston's announcement](https://www.impulsegamer.com/wordpress/?p=25814). Product-family age is not this unit's manufacture date. User's recollection of storage since 2019 suggests at least seven years of calendar age, but is not independently verified. Label/manufacturer serial lookup needed for manufacture date. Exact NAND supplier/revision is unconfirmed.
